# User Guide

## Hello! Welcome to the Minimum Wage Disparities Dashboard!
This interactive application enables users to explore how minimum wages vary across different regions in the United States and compare them against cost-of-living data.

## Data Sources and Attributions
1. Historical Minimum Wage Data (1968–2020): United States Department of Labor.
2. Current Minimum Wage Data: Economic Policy Institute (EPI) Minimum Wage Tracker.
3. Cost of Living Analysis: MIT Living Wage Calculator.

## How to Use the Dashboard
1. **Map View**: Explore wage metrics (Living Wage, Minimum Wage, Wage Gap) across U.S. states.
2. **Bar Chart**: Compare living and minimum wages by state.
3. **Scatterplots**: Visualize the relationship between wage gaps and housing burdens.

## Research Findings and Insights
Key findings include regional wage disparities and the impact of housing burdens on economic inequality.

## Justice, Equality, and Community (JEC) Implications
- Regions with higher housing costs and lower minimum wages create extreme disparities for individuals and families.  
- These inequities disproportionately affect lower-income and marginalized communities, who are overrepresented in minimum wage jobs.
- Housing affordability directly intersects with systemic inequities, particularly impacting racial and ethnic minority groups.
- High housing burdens, coupled with limited wage opportunities, perpetuate economic instability, limiting upward mobility.
- Addressing these disparities requires not only equitable wage reforms but also systemic interventions that consider historical and structural inequalities.
